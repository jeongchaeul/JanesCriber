## 1. Authorized & Intended Use

JanesCriber is an open-source, local AI transcription studio designed exclusively for:

1. **Personal, Non-Commercial Transcription:** Transcribing and indexing speech from user-owned, public, or explicitly authorized audio and video recordings.
2. **Accessibility & Assistive Listening:** Assisting individuals with hearing impairments or meeting accessibility needs through real-time and post-recording transcription.
3. **Academic, Journalistic & Creative Workflow:** Facilitating interview transcription, lecture indexing, subtitle generation, and podcast note-taking under applicable fair use exemptions.

**Users are strictly prohibited from using JanesCriber to violate privacy laws, record or transcribe non-consensual communications where prohibited by jurisdiction (e.g., two-party consent laws), or infringe on intellectual property rights.**

---

## 2. Privacy, Air-Gapping & 100% Local Processing

To ensure complete privacy and operational security:

- **100% Local Inference:** All speech recognition (Whisper, Vosk, Qwen3-ASR, Wav2Vec2) runs strictly on local CPU/GPU hardware.
- **Zero Telemetry:** JanesCriber transmits no user tracking, analytics, media content, audio bytes, or transcription text to any cloud service or third party.
- **Offline Model Storage:** Models are downloaded directly from authoritative upstream open-source repositories and remain entirely local within your configured storage cache.
- **Local Media Security:** Normalized audio buffers and temporary transcription frames are discarded immediately upon job completion in managed cleanup blocks.
